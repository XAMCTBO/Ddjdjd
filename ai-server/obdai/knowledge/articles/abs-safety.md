# ABS, traction and airbag warnings - safety first
tags: ABS, traction, airbag, SRS, C0035, C0040, B0001, wheel speed sensor, brakes, safety

Brake, steering, and airbag systems are safety-critical. An app can explain codes, but a physical inspection by a qualified technician is the only safe next step.

- An ABS or traction light with wheel speed sensor codes (C0035-C0050, or manufacturer C1xxx codes): normal braking usually still works, but ABS and stability control are disabled. Common causes are a damaged sensor cable, debris or rust on the tone ring, or wheel bearing play. Drive gently and get it inspected.
- A red brake warning light, a soft pedal, or a pedal that sinks: stop driving. This can mean low brake fluid or a hydraulic failure.
- An airbag/SRS light: the airbags may not deploy in a crash. Don't probe airbag connectors with a multimeter - accidental deployment can cause injury.
- Generic OBD-II scanners often can't read ABS or SRS modules. Those need enhanced or manufacturer-level access.
