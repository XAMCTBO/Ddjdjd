# Cleaning and testing a MAF sensor
tags: MAF, mass air flow, P0100, P0101, P0171, cleaning, airflow

Rule of thumb: at a warm idle, MAF reads about 2-3 g/s per litre of engine displacement (a 2.0 L engine reads about 4-6 g/s). At full load, grams per second is roughly the engine's horsepower ÷ 1.25. Readings that are consistently low with positive fuel trims indicate a dirty or failing sensor.

Cleaning (about 10 minutes, one can of MAF cleaner):
1. Engine off. Unplug the connector and remove the sensor (usually two screws, often Torx security).
2. Spray only dedicated MAF cleaner on the wires or film element. Never touch the element, and never use brake or carb cleaner.
3. Let it air-dry fully (10+ minutes) before refitting.
4. Fix the cause of contamination: an oiled aftermarket air filter, a torn filter, or a leaking air box.

Clear fuel-trim codes afterwards and watch LTFT over the next sessions. If it returns toward 0 %, the cleaning fixed the problem.
