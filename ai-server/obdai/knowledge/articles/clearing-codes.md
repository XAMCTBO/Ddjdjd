# When to clear trouble codes
tags: clear codes, Mode 04, reset, check engine, MIL

Reading codes is passive and safe. Clearing codes (Mode 04) writes to the engine control unit and:
- erases stored and pending codes and their freeze frames - the evidence of what happened;
- resets every readiness monitor to incomplete, so the car will fail an emissions inspection until a drive cycle completes them;
- doesn't fix anything. If the fault is still present, the code returns.

Clear codes only after a repair, to confirm the fix, or after you've saved a report of the codes and freeze frame. Don't clear codes just before an inspection or a sale. Permanent DTCs (Mode 0A) can't be cleared by a scan tool; they clear themselves once the monitor passes.
