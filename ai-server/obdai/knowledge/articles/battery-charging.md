# Battery and charging system
tags: battery, alternator, voltage, P0562, P0563, U0100, charging, start-stop

The control-module voltage PID (Mode 01 PID 42) gives a quick electrical health check:
- Engine off, after resting: 12.6 V or more is fully charged; 12.2 V is about half charged; below 12.0 V the battery is discharged or failing.
- Engine running: 13.8-14.7 V is normal; smart-charging cars can vary between about 12.5 and 15 V.
- Cranking: a dip below about 9.6 V during cranking indicates a weak battery.

Low voltage causes strange, unrelated codes, especially U-codes (lost communication) and random module faults. Always fix the battery or charging problem first, then clear codes and re-scan. Cars with start-stop need AGM/EFB batteries and often a battery registration step after replacement.
